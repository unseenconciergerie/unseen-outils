01What we handle

Everything that happens once they land.

You keep the client and the relationship. We take care of what happens on the ground, and keep you informed at every step.

A palace suite that never appears online. A table at the restaurant that is full, on the evening they want it. A driver waiting at the aircraft. A seat at the Fashion Week show or the Grand Prix. A private appointment with a maison. And everything nobody planned, handled by the same person, from arrival to departure.
